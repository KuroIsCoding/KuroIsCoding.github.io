      <h2>Biography</h2>
      <p>I am an M.Sc. student at the <a href="https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/">School of Data Science, Fudan University</a>, advised by Prof. Yanghua Xiao and Prof. Deqing Yang in the Knowledge Works Lab. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Nankai University, where I graduated with a GPA of 3.89/4.0 (ranked 7/140).</p>
      <p>My research lies at the intersection of <strong>LLM post-training, reward modeling, and autonomous agents</strong>. I am particularly interested in the feedback loop behind capable agents: how to evaluate open-ended behavior, turn evaluation into reliable learning signals, and enable agents to improve through long-horizon interaction.</p>
      <ol class="research-directions">
        <li><strong>Evaluation and Reward Systems.</strong> I study LLM judges and generative reward models, including their biases, robustness, and use in reinforcement learning. My recent work explores reward systems that diagnose and evolve themselves during training.</li>
        <li><strong>Agent Post-training.</strong> I build research, coding, and work agents that plan, use tools, retain experience, and learn from multi-turn trajectories in realistic environments.</li>
      </ol>
